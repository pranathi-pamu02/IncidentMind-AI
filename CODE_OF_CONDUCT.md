# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in IncidentMind AI to be welcoming, respectful, and constructive for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Give actionable, specific feedback about work rather than making personal attacks.
- Welcome questions and help contributors understand the project.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information, credentials, or private incident data.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and follow maintainer guidance.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, personal insults, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Maintainer response

Project maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and communicated clearly when practical.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the contact options on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/pranathi-pamu02). Do not use public issues for private or safety-sensitive reports. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces and project-related interactions, including issues, pull requests, reviews, and other community channels.
